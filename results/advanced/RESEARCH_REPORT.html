Equity volatility: multi-period modeling evidence

Real measured results, 2020–November 2026 data. Exploratory retrospective portfolio research; no claim of trading profitability or a sustained track record.

Research question

Do range, volume and momentum features add predictive information beyond trailing absolute returns? We compare standardized ridge, quantile-binned boosted stumps, a 16-unit ReLU network, and a validation-selected convex ensemble.

Design

Expanding-window folds test 2023, 2024, 2025, and partial 2026. The previous year selects ensemble weights; earlier data trains candidate models. Models then refit on all pre-test labels. Feature/target crossing split boundaries is purged. Fixed model settings and the experiment plan are saved. Each stock-day receives equal weight in aggregate MAE.

Evidence limits

This is exploratory retrospective research. The earlier project already exposed 2025-2026 outcomes. These years cannot be described as a fresh untouched confirmatory holdout. More model complexity does not guarantee improvement. Intervals are paired circular moving-block bootstrap intervals of daily average MAE gains, not individual-row resampling. They are not adjusted for model comparisons or the broader research process.

Data quality

102 convenience-selected current stocks; selection and survivorship bias remain. Retrospectively adjusted Yahoo OHLC, unchanged provider volume, no independently verified point-in-time corporate actions. Invalid/missing bars are excluded. The target is next observed return and may cross a data gap. Tail observations are retained and listed for investigation; no manual correction or tail exclusion was performed.

Interpretation

Positive bootstrap gain favors the model over rolling-20; an interval spanning zero does not establish a reliable gain. Daily-average bootstrap estimates and stock-day-weighted summary metrics have different weighting. Volatility-only ridge is an ablation, not a causal proof. Test-time tail slices are diagnostics, not information available when predicting. No trading strategy or profit is tested.

Next validation

Freeze a model before collecting genuinely unseen future outcomes; test an independently sourced point-in-time universe; investigate listed extremes and gaps; compare with established model libraries when the runtime permits. Maintain a dated experiment log and repeat evidence across independently motivated projects.
