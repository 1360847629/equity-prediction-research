Important interpretation

The model predicts next close-to-close direction, but a signal computed after the close cannot earn that day-to-next-open gap at the earlier close. The executable proxy therefore earns only next open-to-close returns. The equal-weight intraday comparator matches that exposure window. SPY buy-and-hold includes overnight returns and is contextual, not exposure matched. Daily OHLC prices do not demonstrate actual auction or broker fills. No liquidity capacity, market-impact estimation, taxes, share rounding, minimum broker fees, or intraday stop fills are modeled. No shorting or borrow costs apply. Maximum drawdown is an observed historical result, not a guaranteed loss limit.

Convenience-universe selection, survivorship bias, retrospective adjusted prices and previously inspected test periods remain. The 55% rule was fixed for this experiment, not independently validated. Cost scenarios are sensitivity checks, not an optimization over test profits. Do not interpret favorable returns as proven trading skill or readiness for real capital.

Year-boundary sessions without saved predictions are retained in the daily calendar. Stock strategies hold cash on those sessions; SPY benchmarks retain their specified exposure. Missing-signal dates are listed below.
